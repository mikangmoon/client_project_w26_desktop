Network and Connections

Utilize Your Network

Networking is the process of building professional relationships to exchange information and support. It is a very useful tool when finding jobs, learning about industries, and gaining personal relationships to existing professionals.

TIARA Method for Informational Interview/Coffee Chat

A step-by-step approach to building likability with others and also a guide to confidently handling the Q&A portion of any informational interview.

  • Trends: Tailor trend-related questions to the specific company, industry, or role of the person you’re interviewing.
  • Insights: Use insight questions to narrow the focus to the individual. Ask about their daily routine, career history, and specific professional experiences.
  • Advice: Asking for advice brings the conversation back to you.
  • Resources: Request recommendations for tools, blogs, and digital resources. This initiates the closing phase of the interview while establishing specific touchpoints for future follow-up.
  • Assignments: Asking about assignment questions can give you another reason to follow up the interviewee.
